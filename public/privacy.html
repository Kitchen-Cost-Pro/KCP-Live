Privacy and browser storage

Updated 27 July 2026

KCP uses essential cookies and browser storage needed to provide secure sign-in, remember your workspace and display preferences, protect in-progress work, and operate the application reliably.

What KCP stores

  • Authentication and security session information.
  • Workspace, theme, dashboard, navigation, and notification preferences.
  • Temporary in-progress drafts used to prevent accidental work loss.
  • A record that the essential-storage notice has been acknowledged.

What KCP does not use this for

KCP does not use the essential-storage notice to enable advertising cookies or cross-site advertising tracking. Blocking essential browser storage may prevent sign-in, saved preferences, or parts of the application from working correctly.
